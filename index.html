02 / A little context
“The best way to understand an idea is to try building it.”
A principle I keep returning to.

Not just interested in technology. Interested in what it can change.

I’m a Diploma student in Computer Science Engineering, currently exploring how software, AI and hardware can work together to solve practical problems. I enjoy moving between disciplines — from building a web interface to thinking through a search pipeline, a computer-vision model or the architecture of a specialised chip.

I don’t see learning as something that ends with a syllabus. I learn by taking an idea apart, researching the pieces, making a working version and improving what doesn’t work. I care about the details: a clear user journey, reliable logic, useful feedback and technology that feels approachable.

My long-term direction is to become an engineer who can move comfortably between product thinking and deep technical implementation — and contribute to work that is genuinely useful.
